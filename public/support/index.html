Lean Lah! for iPhone

Something not working, or an idea? Email krishsaraf05@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you tapped before it went wrong.

My rings, steps, or sleep don't show

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Lean Lah!, and turn on the data you want to see. Apple doesn't tell apps whether read access is on, so Lean Lah! can only show what Health shares.

Photo estimates or voice logs say to sign in

These need an account. Go to Health → Settings and sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Each account gets 20 photo estimates and 60 voice logs a day.

An estimate looks wrong

Estimates are a starting point. Every number can be edited before you save, and later by tapping the meal.

Voice logs don't hear me

Speech is recognised on your iPhone. Check that Lean Lah! has microphone and speech recognition access in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Deleting your account

Go to Health → Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what's removed.
