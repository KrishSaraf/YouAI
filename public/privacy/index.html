Privacy policy

Lean Lah! for iPhone · Last updated 11 October 2026

This policy explains what Lean Lah! collects, where it goes, and how to delete it. In short: what you log is saved to your account so it's there when you sign in again, photos and spoken text go to an AI service only when you ask for an estimate, and nothing is used for ads or sold.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Apple Health readings. Lean Lah! reads your activity rings, steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, and weight from Apple Health to show them in the app. These readings are not uploaded.
  • Voice recordings. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The audio never leaves it.

What's saved to your account

When you sign in, the following is stored with Supabase, our database provider, so you can get it back on another iPhone:

  • Your email address, a user ID, and, if you sign in with Google, the name on that Google account.
  • Meals you log: name, time, calories, protein, carbs, fat, and a small copy of the photo if you added one.
  • Workouts, habits and the days you ticked them, and exercises you added.
  • Weight, water, sleep, and vitals (blood pressure, temperature, blood oxygen) that you enter in the app.
  • Your weight unit setting, and how many photo and voice estimates you used each day.

Weight, water, sleep, vitals, meals, and workouts you enter are also written to Apple Health on your iPhone, if you allow it.

AI estimates

Only after you agree in the app, and only when you tap to estimate, Lean Lah! sends:

  • the photo of a meal or gym equipment, or
  • the text of what you said for a spoken log.

It goes to our server, hosted by Netlify, which passes it to OpenRouter to run Google's Gemini model. The result comes back to you to check before anything is saved. Our server doesn't keep what was sent. OpenRouter and Google handle it under their own privacy policies. You can turn AI estimates off in Settings at any time.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Deleting your data

  • One log: delete it in the app. Its contents are removed from your account. A blank marker stays so your other devices know to delete their copy too.
  • Everything: go to Health → Settings → Delete account. Your account and everything saved to it are deleted from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, Lean Lah! is also disconnected from your Apple ID.
  • Logs stored only on your iPhone stay until you delete them or delete the app. Data written to Apple Health stays there until you remove it in the Health app.

Children

Lean Lah! isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Not medical advice

Lean Lah! is for general fitness tracking. Its estimates can be wrong and aren't medical or dietary advice.
